Why BASALT

Three principles, one system.

01 — CHEMISTRY

Cobalt-free, by default.

LFP is not a marketing upgrade. It is a different thermal and lifetime profile — lower energy density, more predictable behavior, no cobalt or nickel in the cathode.

02 — MEASURED

Documented like an instrument.

Usable capacity, measured noise, charge times and cell-monitoring details are published upfront. No "up to" marketing that doesn't hold up to a stopwatch.

03 — EXPANDABLE

One cell, three form factors.

From trailhead to whole-home backup, the same LFP chemistry and monitoring architecture. No proprietary black box, no dead ends if you need more later.

Designed toward these standards. Not certified. No certificate numbers, listings or approvals are claimed.

UL 2743 (portable power), UL 9540 / UL 9540A (energy storage system), UL 1973 (battery cells), IEEE 1547 (grid interconnection), NEC 480 / 702 (install) — design directions only.

Use cases

A system for three kinds of ground.

Trail

1.4 kWh usable, 37 lb. For fridge, lights, chargers, and a quiet space heater. DC solar input means no inverter at camp.

Road

6.0 kWh usable, wheels included. Van builds, film crews, remote tool operation. 3,000 W continuous for compressors and welders.

Home

Start at 3.0 kWh, expand to 9.6 kWh with BATT modules. Essential fridge, well pump, and medical devices through multi-day outages.
